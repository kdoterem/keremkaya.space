my writing reflected the many state my soul mind and body been in.
many fractures fractured my sentences, punctuated tire, fluctuating love.
many times i've sat at the deck and rode out a thought and it fluctuated long before i could follow it the way it deserves to actualization.
many times i treated as if it was actualized,
ESPECIALLY, when it wasn't.
i take a drag from my cigarette
get lost in a word choice i made,
and decorate around it.
all the little impulses, the sounds that are followed
we demonstrated in earlier poems that you are a follower. follower of the sounds, thoughts and things that arise. 
and then i choose
i choose not to be dictated by things and lead 
cause my poems also said lead never follow
don't choose which is witch,
choose what to feel.
