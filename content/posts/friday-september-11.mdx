when i need to be going somewhere,
i wind up somewhere else.
taking a path unemerged,
virgin lands not so virgin,
lying the ruins of those that lied,
the stench of iron still lingers.
it evokes something red,
those that died are now dead.
lingers still, ailment of the ill,
still child, unkind lies,
ahead, kind awaits,
below kind lies.
all this goes somewhere,
my scent attracts hunters, i'm not a prey.
